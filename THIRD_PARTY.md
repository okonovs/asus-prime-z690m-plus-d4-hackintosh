## Not redistributed

- **HfsPlus.efi and OcBinaryData Resources:** obtain directly from [OcBinaryData](https://github.com/acidanthera/OcBinaryData).
- **USBToolBox.kext 1.2.0:** obtain directly from its [upstream release](https://github.com/USBToolBox/kext/releases/tag/1.2.0). No explicit redistribution licence was found in the inspected repository.
- **MemTest86 and CFGLock:** optional diagnostics omitted; not required to boot this configuration.
- **macOS:** obtain from Apple; no installer or Apple account identity is included.
